Project Details

Joint Path Selection and Intersection Scheduling

Joint route selection and shared-intersection scheduling for multiple robots in a warehouse traffic network.

Network and Candidate Paths

Cost Function

Decision variables: route choice \(Z\) and intersection schedule \(U\).

Objective

\[ \min_{Z,U}\; J =\sum_{n=1}^{N} \left\{ \sum_{k=1}^{K_n(Z_n)}\delta_{n,k}(U) +\left(T_n^{\mathrm{sel}}(Z_n)-T_n^{\mathrm{sp}}\right) \right\}. \]

Total contention delay + extra free-flow travel time relative to the shortest candidate path.

Constraints

Robot motion and operating limits

\[ \dot{x}_n(t)=v_n(t),\qquad \dot{v}_n(t)=a_n(t), \] \[ 0\le v_n(t)\le v_{\max},\qquad -a_{\max}\le a_n(t)\le a_{\max}. \]

Select exactly one next intersection

\[ \sum_{j\in\mathcal S_n(t)}z_{n,(i,j)}(t)=1, \qquad z_{n,(i,j)}(t)\in\{0,1\}. \]

Task timing along the selected route

\[ \gamma_{n,k}=\beta_{n,k}+C_{n,k}, \] \[ \alpha_{n,k}=\gamma_{n,k-1}+\Delta_{n,k-1}, \qquad k\in\{2,\ldots,K_n(Z_n)\}, \] \[ \delta_{n,k}=\beta_{n,k}-\alpha_{n,k}\ge 0. \]

Here \(\alpha_{n,k}\), \(\beta_{n,k}\), and \(\gamma_{n,k}\) are the task-generation, actual-start, and completion times; \(C_{n,k}\) is the intersection traversal time, and \(\Delta_{n,k-1}\) is the minimum feasible travel time between consecutive intersections.

Mutual-exclusion scheduling

\[ u_{n,i}(t)\in\{0,1\},\qquad \sum_{n=1}^{N}u_{n,i}(t)\le 1,\qquad \sum_{i=1}^{I}u_{n,i}(t)\le 1. \]

Each intersection is occupied by at most one robot at a time, and each robot occupies at most one intersection at a time.

Co-Design Example

5 vehicles · 9–12 candidate routes per vehicle · 19 scheduled intersection tasks.
