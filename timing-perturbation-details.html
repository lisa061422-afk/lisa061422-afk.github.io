Timing Perturbations · Tube-Based MPC

Linear Systems with Timing Perturbations

Paper: R. Wang and N. Yao, “Robust Model Predictive Control for Networked Control Systems with Timing Perturbations,” ACC 2024.

A tube-based MPC architecture compensates for delayed control updates by combining a nominal trajectory with a timing-dependent feedback correction.

Dynamics

Plant, nominal center, and timing mismatch

The nominal model defines the center of the tube. A delayed task completion creates a measurable timing perturbation and a deviation from that center.

\[ \dot{x}_i(t)=A_i x_i(t)+B_i u_i(t),\qquad \dot{x}_i^{n}(t)=A_i x_i^{n}(t)+B_i u_i^{n}(t), \] \[ \Delta_i[k]=\gamma_i[k]-\tilde{\gamma}_i[k]\geq 0. \]
